Common Questions

FAQ

Do you need my water and power?

Yes. I need access to an outdoor water spigot and a standard power outlet at the location. If there's no water source available, I'm not able to complete the job, so please confirm before booking.

How long does a detail take?

Basic Wash runs 1.5 to 2 hours. Full Interior runs 2.5 to 3 hours. Full Detail runs 3.5 to 4 hours, and can go 5 to 6 on vehicles that need extra work.

What happens if it rains?

The job gets rescheduled at no charge. I'll reach out to find a new time that works.

Can you detail at an apartment complex?

Yes, as long as there's an accessible water source and outlet at the parking area.

Do you do trucks and SUVs?

Yes. Larger vehicles run about 10 to 20 percent more depending on size and condition.

How far in advance should I book?

48 hours notice is ideal so I can confirm the slot and plan the route.
